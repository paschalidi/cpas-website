# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People who might want to hire Christos: recruiters, engineering managers, and potential clients evaluating him for freelance or full-time roles. They browse quickly, often on mobile, looking for signal of craft and product thinking. Many will arrive from LinkedIn, GitHub, or a referral.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that demonstrates technical depth and product intuition through the site's own design, while surfacing a blog that proves sustained product thinking over time. The interface itself is evidence of the craft being sold. Success = a visitor who reaches out to hire.

## Brand Personality

Technical, playful, crafted, product-minded, content-driven.

The site should feel like it was built by someone who *could* build your next product, not someone who downloaded a template. Playful moments (the custom cursor, the cheeky email copy) reward attention without undermining professionalism. The blog is not an afterthought — it's proof of sustained product thinking.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS-template portfolios (Inter font, purple gradients, "I'm a passionate developer" hero)
- Cream/beige AI-default backgrounds that read as 2026 slop
- Cookie-cutter card grids with icon + heading + text repeated identically
- Boring "hire me" pages with no point of view
- Overly corporate or sterile developer portfolios that hide personality

## Design Principles

1. **The site is the resume.** Every interaction, animation, and layout decision should demonstrate the craft being sold. If the portfolio feels templated, the message fails.
2. **Playful moments reward attention.** The custom cursor, the aurora shader, the cheeky email body — these are Easter eggs that prove there's a human behind the code, not noise for its own sake.
3. **Content proves depth.** The blog is front-and-center signal, not a buried footer link. Sustained writing about product and engineering is the differentiator.
4. **Every pixel is deliberate.** No default Tailwind spacing, no arbitrary margins, no "looks fine" compromises. The level of finish should hold up to a design-studio review.
5. **Dark by default, light by intention.** The black background with aurora shader is the identity. Any departure from dark needs a scene-level justification.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum for all text contrast
- Custom cursor must not break keyboard navigation or focus visibility
- Reduced motion `@media` support for aurora and entrance animations
- Focus indicators visible and styled consistently with the dark theme
- Touch targets ≥ 44×44px on mobile
